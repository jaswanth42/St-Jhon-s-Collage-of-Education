ABOUT BACHELOR OF EDUCATION (B. Ed)

The bachelor's of education popularly referred to as B. Ed is a two year full time degree course that is essential for candidates who are interested in pursuing a career in teaching and other related fields. The students in the B. Ed course are given professional training in both Arts and Science subjects from the teaching point of view. B. Ed is mandatory for all those who want to teach in primary and secondary schools.

About Us

Student Performance

Since its inception in 1999, the college has proudly admitted over 2,200 students, achieving a remarkable 97% pass rate, with an average intake of 120 students per year.

Student Placement

Our students have excelled across various sectors, with over 811 securing positions as government teachers, 500+ in other government roles, and 1,400+ thriving in the private sector.

classrooms
