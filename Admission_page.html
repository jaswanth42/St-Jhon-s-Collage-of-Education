Bachelor of Education (B.Ed) Program

About Bachelor of Education (B.Ed)

The Bachelor of Education, popularly referred to as B.Ed, is a two-year full-time degree course essential for candidates interested in pursuing a career in teaching and related fields. The program provides professional training in both Arts and Science subjects from a teaching perspective. B.Ed is mandatory for those who wish to teach in primary and secondary schools.

B.Ed Admission Process

The admission process in the B.Ed program is both entrance-based and merit-based. Candidates appearing for the B.Ed entrance examination must undergo a counseling round for final seat allocation. The entrance exam primarily includes objective-type questions on subjects like:

  • English
  • General Knowledge
  • Aptitude
  • Basic Arithmetic
  • Teaching Potential
  • Local Knowledge

There will be a counseling round after the merit list is announced.
